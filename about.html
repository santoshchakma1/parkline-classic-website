Our story

A classic, kept simple, since 2019

The Parkline Classic started as a straightforward promise: a clean room and a good plate of food, available whenever you need them.

Indranagar, Agartala

How we started

Built around one neighbourhood

The Parkline Classic opened its doors in Indranagar, Agartala, with a simple idea: not every guest needs a resort — most just need a comfortable room, a decent meal, and people at the desk who pick up the phone at 3 a.m. as readily as at 3 p.m.

That's shaped everything since — from keeping the kitchen running around the clock, to training the front desk to treat a midnight check-in the same as a midday one. The seal on our sign, stamped "Est. 2019," is a small reminder of that promise every time a guest walks in.

What guides us

Three things we don't compromise on

Warm, unhurried hospitality

Every guest is greeted the same way, whether they've booked ahead or just walked in off the street.

Genuinely round-the-clock

Front desk, housekeeping and kitchen are staffed 24 hours — not "24 hours, mostly."

Honest, classic cooking

A multi-cuisine menu kept to dishes done well, rather than an endless list done half-heartedly.
